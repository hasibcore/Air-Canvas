Why AirCanvas Pro?

Everything you need to draw wirelessly with Wacom-level precision

🪄
1-Euro Adaptive Filter
Velocity-based adaptive filtering eliminates digitizer jitter and hand tremors while preserving sharp corners with zero lag.
⭕
1:1 Shape Geometry
Dynamic monitor aspect ratio match guarantees circles drawn on mobile render as 100% perfect circles on PC without distortion.
🎚️
Calibrated Pressure Curves
Standard (1:1), Soft (Gamma 0.7 for light touches), and Firm (Gamma 1.4 for delicate sketching) pressure profiles.
⚡
Ultra-Low Latency
Binary WebSocket protocol delivers <5ms average input latency on a local Wi-Fi network.
🔒
Secure PIN Auth
4-digit PIN challenge-response with rotating session keys protects every connection.
🖥️
Sub-Pixel Pen Injection
Injects real Win32 touch & stylus events with 1024 pressure levels, works with Photoshop, Blender, and Krita.
