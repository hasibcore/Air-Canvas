How It Works

Seamless cross-platform client-server architecture.

1. Mobile Client

Acts as your high-precision tablet. Captures pressure, tilt, and palm-rejected strokes.

2. Local WiFi

Streams data via custom 32-byte UDP/WebSocket binary protocol at lightning speeds.

3. Desktop Server

Receives data and injects synthetic native pen input into your favorite creative apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Does it work with Photoshop and Blender?

Yes! Because Air Canvas injects native OS pen events, it works out of the box with any app that supports graphics tablets, including Photoshop, Illustrator, Krita, and Blender.

Do I need an active internet connection?

No. Air Canvas operates entirely over your local WiFi network. No data leaves your home, ensuring zero lag from internet routing.

What happens if I don't have a stylus?

You can still use Air Canvas with your finger! However, pressure and tilt features are only available if your device supports them (e.g., S-Pen or Apple Pencil).

Is there a cable option?

Currently, Air Canvas is optimized for wireless, low-latency performance using your local network router. USB tethering is not yet natively supported but can work via local network sharing.
